To My Dearest sister 💗

Happy Raksha Bandhan!

Happy Raksha Bandhan Shrushti Didi! 🩷🎀 Having you as my sister makes life a little more beautiful and a lot more fun.

ou have such a sweet, innocent soul that makes you truly special. From your silly talks to your tiny little moments...

Five years ago, we met as complete strangers because you were my mama’s neighbour , and honestly, my first impression of you was that you had quite a bit of attitude and maybe a little ego too 😭 . But after we started talking and I got to know you better, I realised that my first impression was completely wrong. You are actually a very kind, soft-hearted and caring person, and over time I got to know you more through our conversations and the little moments we shared. We celebrated Diwali together, then Bhaubij too, and slowly those simple moments became some really nice memories. 🪔✨ I think you were around your 11th standard at that time, and as time passed, our bond naturally became stronger and more comfortable. We may not have known each other since childhood, and we may have met only around five years ago, but I'm genuinely happy that our paths crossed. ❤️ For me, you are a very special person and , more importantly, someone I can proudly call my Didi.🤍 I really appreciate the support, guidance and positivity you have given me along the way, and I'm glad that our bond became what it is today—simple, genuine and like a brother-sister bond.🌸 On this Raksha Bandhan, I just wanted to put into words how much I value this bond and all the memories we've made. You are one of the supporters in my life, and I'm really grateful for that. Wishing you a very Happy Raksha Bandhan, Shrushti Didi! 🪢✨ — By Varad ❤️! 🫶🏻🌸

Love you always! 💗
